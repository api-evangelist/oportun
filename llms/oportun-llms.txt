# Oportun

> Oportun (Nasdaq: OPRT) is a mission-driven fintech and U.S. Treasury-certified CDFI providing responsible, affordable personal loans, savings, and budgeting tools to people with limited or no credit history. It also runs an Enterprise Platform offering Lending-as-a-Service (LaaS) and embedded-finance partnerships. No public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, SDKs, or self-serve API is published; the LaaS integration API is offered only through contact-gated enterprise partnerships.
